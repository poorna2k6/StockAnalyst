Beginners Corner
What is a stock? A stock is a small piece of ownership in a company. If the company grows and profits, your stock value goes up.
What is an ETF? An ETF (like SPY or QQQ) is a basket of many stocks in one. It's safer for beginners because you're diversified automatically.
Golden rule: Never invest money you can't afford to lose for at least 1 year. Start with ETFs before individual stocks.
