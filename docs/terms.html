7. Privacy & Data Storage

Anonymous use (no account)

  • All portfolio data, watchlists, alerts, and settings are stored locally in your browser's localStorage only.
  • No personal data is collected or transmitted to any server.
  • Your API keys are stored only on your device.

Signed-in use (optional account)

  • When you create an account, your portfolio positions, watchlist items, and price alerts are synced to a Supabase PostgreSQL database so you can access them across devices.
  • Your AI API keys are stored encrypted using Supabase Vault (AES-256) and are never stored in plaintext.
  • We collect your email address (used solely for authentication and transactional notifications).
  • AI-generated portfolio reports are stored temporarily in Supabase Storage as PDFs and accessible only to you via a time-limited signed URL.
  • Daily portfolio value snapshots are stored for performance chart history.
  • Activity related to AI investment recommendations is logged in an immutable audit log to satisfy SEC record-keeping requirements (SEC Rule 204-2). These logs are retained for the life of your account.

8. Your GDPR Rights (EU/UK Residents)

If you are located in the European Union, United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction that provides data rights under GDPR or equivalent legislation, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You may request a full export of all data we hold about you. Use the "Export My Data" button in Settings → Account.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request deletion of all your personal data. Use the "Delete My Account" button in Settings → Account. This permanently removes your profile, portfolio data, watchlist, alerts, API keys, Storage files, and Stripe billing records. It cannot be undone.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update your email and display name via your account settings at any time.
  • Right to Portability: The data export (see above) is provided in machine-readable JSON format, enabling portability.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may sign out at any time. All data operations cease and the app reverts to local-only mode.
  • Right to Object: To object to any data processing, contact us via our GitHub repository. You may also delete your account at any time to stop all processing.

To exercise your rights, use the in-app tools in Settings → Account or contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository. We will respond within 30 days.

Legal basis for processing: We process your data based on your consent (account creation) and our legitimate interest in providing the service. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Data transfers: Your data is processed by Supabase (infrastructure hosted in the EU/US) and Stripe (payment processing). Both are GDPR-compliant processors operating under Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.
