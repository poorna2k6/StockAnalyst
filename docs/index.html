Stock Trading Income

AI picks specific stocks and trading strategies to earn from the market — tailored to your style and portfolio.

Strategies At a Glance
Dividend Income
SCHD, VYM, JEPI, O, REALTY — pay you every quarter just for holding. Yield 3-8% annually with little effort.
Covered Calls
Own 100+ shares? Sell monthly call options and collect premium — earn 1-3% extra per month on holdings like AAPL, TSLA, MSFT.
Swing Trading
Buy oversold stocks near support, sell at resistance in 3-14 days. Target 3-8% per trade. Works well with earnings catalysts.
Cash-Secured Puts
Sell put options on stocks you'd want to buy. Collect premium upfront. If assigned, you buy shares at a discount.
T-Bills & Money Market
Park idle cash in SGOV or BIL — earning ~4-5% APY risk-free while you wait for the right trade.
